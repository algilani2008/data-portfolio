- The 21 Arab League countries with data back to 1960 together grew from 91.5 million people in 1960 to 497.9 million in 2025: 5.4x, versus 2.7x for the world. Their share of humanity rose from 3.0% to 6.1% (not included, series starts later: Palestine).
- Fastest growth since 1960: United Arab Emirates (88x), Qatar (83x) and Kuwait (16x). Slowest: Tunisia (2.9x).

- The six GCC states held 3.9% of the population of those 21 countries in 1960 and 12.7% in 2025.
- Fastest average growth over the last 10 years (2015-2025): Somalia (3.59%/yr), United Arab Emirates (3.07%/yr). Slowest: Lebanon (-1.01%/yr).
